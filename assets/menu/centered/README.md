# Centered menu photos

These five images were edited from the user-supplied photos in the parent folder with the built-in imagegen tool. The app uses these edited copies; the originals are retained in `assets/menu/`.

Each delivered photo is 1254 x 1254 pixels, with a square 1:1 canvas and the dish centered. AI editing can reconstruct details when extending the framing.

## Prompt used for each image

Edit target: the supplied food photograph. Primary request: reframe to a precisely square 1:1 restaurant menu photograph, 1024x1024. Center the main serving vessel both horizontally and vertically and show its complete silhouette with roughly 10% breathing room, occupying about 80% of the available frame along its longest dimension. Preserve the original dish's identity, plating, natural photographic appearance, colors and camera angle; change only framing and extend the existing background where needed. Do not invent new food, change the recipe, add text, logos or watermark. No borders, no collage, no stretching. Output a single square edited photograph.

Dish-specific additions:

- `adobo.png`: Chicken Adobo in its brown bowl: retain the original chicken pieces, bay leaves, glossy sauce and warm tabletop/yellow cloth.
- `sinigang.png`: Sinigang na Baboy in its metal pot: retain the original pork, tamarind broth and vegetables, metal handles and warm backdrop.
- `kare_kare.png`: Kare-Kare in its dark bowl: retain the original oxtail/beef pieces, orange peanut sauce and green vegetables, yellow cloth and wooden tabletop.
- `pancit_canton.png`: Pancit Canton on its wooden plate: retain the original noodles, shrimp, chicken, sausage and vegetables, blue checked tablecloth.
- `halo_halo.png`: Halo-Halo in its tall glass and white saucer: retain all colorful layers, purple ice cream, toppings, spoon and softly blurred green background. Keep the entire glass, top of ice cream, stem and saucer visible.
